# Research Commercialisation — shared guardrails

The general guardrails (disclaimer, no invented figures, premise checking, the
principle of keeping maturity as an internal note) are collected in one place: **see
`../../meta/shared-guardrails.md` — read it first.** This file contains only
what is genuinely pack-specific here.

---

## Disclaimer for this pack

In addition to the general disclaimer (`shared-guardrails.md`): the choice of
pathway (spin-out vs. licensing), funding strategy, equity split, or any other
commercialisation decision is always the responsibility of the researcher/team
and their TTO/legal counsel.

## No inventing figures or facts — even outside the anchor handbook

This pack's skills are anchored in the owner's own published handbook (*the
owner's published commercialisation guide*) — this raises reliability compared
to a pure research-level scaffold, but it does not remove the responsibility:

1. **Handbook content** (e.g. TRL definitions, funding programme names, equity
   benchmarks, case examples) can be used as-is — cite the source
   (`references/sources.md`).
2. **The user's own baseline data** (e.g. their own organisation's IP policy,
   their own equity split, their own budget) — use it, don't fill gaps with a
   guess.
3. **Currency** — funding programmes (EIC Accelerator, SBIR/STTR, tax
   incentives, etc.) and their terms change. The handbook's figures are from
   2025; if the user needs current information on a specific programme, direct
   them to check it directly with the funder rather than presenting the
   handbook's figure as present-day fact.

## Premise checking in this pack

If a fact the user states (e.g. "my university doesn't require an invention
disclosure," "we don't have a TTO") is material to the outcome but deviates
from the handbook's general model, surface it instead of quietly continuing on
an assumption — institutional practices vary significantly (see
`skills/ip-disclosure-and-ownership-check/SKILL.md`).

## Pack-specific note

Does not give legal, tax, or investment advice. On IP, contract, and
employment law questions, always direct the user to their own organisation's
TTO, lawyer, or specialist — if needed, use separate contract, corporate,
employment law, or AI regulation expertise alongside this (not included in
this skills pack).
