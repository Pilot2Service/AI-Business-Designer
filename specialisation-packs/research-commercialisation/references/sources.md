# Sources

## Primary source

**The owner's published commercialisation guide** — part of the owner's
toolkit, © 2025 the owner. A 75-page handbook written by the owner of this
specialisation pack: a business designer and venture architect with over 15
years of experience, having led approximately 500 commercialisation projects
with startups, universities, and companies across Europe and Asia. This is
the primary anchor for the entire `research-commercialisation` pack
(`source_layer: owner`).

**AFCA — Founder's Competence Assessment** — the owner's own self-assessment
tool, part of the "Tools for Researchers" series. See `afca-framework.md`.

## The handbook's own source references (extracted from context, not an exhaustive list)

These are sources cited by the handbook itself — use them to trace claims
back to the original research; do not cite them as if this repo's own
research had independently confirmed them.

- *The future of European competitiveness. A competitiveness strategy for Europe.* 2025.
- *UK Spinout Report 2024* (Independent Review of University Spin-out
  Companies, gov.uk).
- *Spin-offs: EU27, 2025.*
- Gao et al., 2023 — patenting and commercialisation rates at Chinese
  universities.
- NSF Indicators, 2024 — invention disclosures and spin-outs at US
  universities.
- University of Michigan, 2024 — annual invention/patent/spin-out figures.
- Ries, 2011 — *The Lean Startup*.
- Ziakis, 2022; Sperber & Linder, 2022 — the effect of entrepreneurial
  ecosystems on early-stage performance.
- Finerva, 2022 — trends in global VC funding.
- Foley & Lardner, 2025 — Corporate Venture Capital (CVC) statistics.
- Wikipedia: TRL — the history and use of the Technology Readiness Level.
- Hewitt-Dundas et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024; Kirihata research — the
  TTO's role in spin-out survival.
- Davey et al., 2024 — the effect of university-industry collaboration on
  spin-out formation.
- EIC Impact Report 2025 — the funding impact of the European Innovation
  Council.
- USIT guidance (ten-u.org) — benchmark ranges for spin-out terms in life
  sciences.
- Colombo & Grilli, 2005; Choi et al., 2019; Denoo et al., 2024 — the effect
  of founding team composition on performance.
- Perkmann et al., 2013; Robin & Schubert, 2013 — the effect of
  university-industry collaboration on commercialisation.
- Eesley & Roberts, 2012 — the resilience of mission-driven founders.
- Christensen, 1997 — *The Innovator's Dilemma*, disruption theory.
- Demola (demola.net) — a cross-disciplinary student project model.
