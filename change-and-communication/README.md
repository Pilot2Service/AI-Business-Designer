# Change & Communication

Change management, facilitation, and executive communication amid uncertainty.

## Skills in this pack

| Skill | Description |
|---|---|
| `stakeholder-communication-plan` | Builds a communication plan for different stakeholders during a change. |
| `stakeholder-pressure-and-information-gap-mapping` | Maps the information asymmetry between a team and a decision-maker before a pitch is written — what pressures and constraints they're actually operating under, surfaced through pointed diagnostic questions. |
| `workshop-and-facilitation-design` | Designs the structure of a cross-functional workshop or design-thinking session. |
| `executive-narrative-and-storyline` | Turns analysis into a narrative and storyline that serves executive decision-making. |
| `whiteboard-clarity-and-jargon-stripping` | Strips AI-generated corporate jargon ("AI slop") out of a text or pitch using a slop-strip test, a whiteboard test, and Steve-Jobs-style one-line compression — a register pass, complementary to the logical structuring in `executive-narrative-and-storyline`. |
| `benefits-realisation-tracking` | Tracks and reports on the realisation of promised benefits after implementation. |

## Suggested flow for a high-stakes pitch or business case

```
stakeholder-pressure-and-information-gap-mapping
   (understand what the decision-maker actually needs to hear)
              │
              ▼
executive-narrative-and-storyline
   (structure the argument — SCQA, MECE, evidence)
              │
              ▼
whiteboard-clarity-and-jargon-stripping
   (strip jargon, compress to one clear line, before it ships)
```
