# Strategic Thinking

Systemic, hypothesis-driven strategic thinking (Liedtka 1998; McKinsey MECE /
issue trees).

## Skills in this pack

| Skill | Description |
|---|---|
| `systems-mapping` | Maps the full value-creating system and its dependencies before you design a solution. |
| `strategic-intent-framing` | Frames a clear strategic intent that focuses energy and cuts out noise. |
| `hypothesis-driven-strategy` | Structures a strategic problem with MECE and an issue tree, then tests the hypotheses against data. |
| `scenario-and-foresight` | Builds alternative future scenarios and stress-tests a strategy against them. |
| `strategic-options-evaluation` | Compares strategic options in a structured way (impact/effort, risk, resources) to support a decision. |
| `second-and-third-order-effects-mapping` | Anticipates the second- and third-order effects of a decision or AI solution on customer behavior, competition, and the organization. |

## Anchored in

- Liedtka (1998) — hypothesis-driven
- Liedtka (1998) — intelligent opportunism
- Liedtka (1998) — intent-focused
- Liedtka (1998) — systems perspective
- Liedtka (1998) — thinking in time
- Hamel & Prahalad (1989), "Strategic Intent," *Harvard Business Review* — the
  original source for the term this pack's intent-framing skill operationalizes
- Brandenburger & Nalebuff (1996), *Co-opetition* — the Value Net, a concrete
  structure (customers, suppliers, competitors, complementors around a focal
  actor) used to operationalize Liedtka's systems perspective in
  `systems-mapping`
- Conn & McLean (2018), *Bulletproof Problem Solving* — a published, detailed
  account of McKinsey-style hypothesis-driven, MECE/issue-tree problem solving
- Schwartz (1991), *The Art of the Long View* — the classic scenario-planning
  method (drivers of change → critical uncertainties → a small set of
  divergent scenarios), used to operationalize `scenario-and-foresight`
- Systems thinking / "second-order thinking" technique in strategic
  decision-making; a research report on AI Business Designer skills for the
  age of AI, supplied by the pack owner (2026)
