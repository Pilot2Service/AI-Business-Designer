# Task starter: Business Model Canvas Diagnostic

**When to use:** You (or a client) has a filled-in Business Model Canvas and
you need to know honestly whether it's sound — not just that all nine boxes
have something written in them.

**What to provide:** The canvas contents, box by box (paste or attach).

**Start with this:**

1. "Use the bmc-canvas-diagnostic-reading skill to review this canvas:
   [paste canvas]. Check it against the known failure patterns and tell me
   the 2–3 weakest boxes and why."
2. "Match this canvas against the innovation pattern library using
   bmc-innovation-pattern-matching — which 3–5 known patterns does it
   resemble, and what does that suggest we're missing?"
3. "Assume this canvas is more fragile than it looks. Use
   bmc-operational-risk-scanning and bmc-revenue-quality-scoring to find
   where the revenue or delivery model would break under stress."

**What you get:** A diagnostic read, not a redraw — specific weak boxes
named, revenue quality and operational risk flagged, and a small set of
comparable innovation patterns to check the model against, each citing which
established heuristic is being applied.

**What happens next:** Fix the weak boxes; if you're facilitating a live
session next, use `bmc-session-facilitation-design` to plan it instead of
running it ad hoc.
