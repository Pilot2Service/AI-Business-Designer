# Playbook: AI Initiative Scoping

Chains skills together from identifying an AI initiative to an approved,
responsible implementation plan.

1. `data-strategy-and-literacy/skills/data-role-diagnosis` (optional,
   recommended if data's role is unclear — enabler vs. strategic asset —
   affects how Data Readiness is scored in the next step)
2. `ai-strategy-and-governance/skills/ai-opportunity-portfolio`
3. `ai-strategy-and-governance/skills/ai-use-case-feasibility-and-poc-scoping`
4. `prototyping-and-demonstration/skills/demo-framing-and-expectation-setting`
   (frame the PoC/Pilot/MVP terminology and the "proves/doesn't prove" pair
   before prototyping)
5. `prototyping-and-demonstration/skills/rapid-prototype-and-vibe-coding-craft`
   and `prototyping-and-demonstration/skills/demo-delivery-and-storytelling`
   (if the initiative needs a concrete demo before approval)
6. `ai-strategy-and-governance/skills/responsible-ai-and-governance-check`
   (deeper EU AI Act analysis requires separate regulatory
   expertise/plugin — not included in this skills pack)
7. `prototyping-and-demonstration/skills/demo-to-business-case-bridge` →
   `business-case-and-analysis/skills/business-case-builder`
8. `ai-strategy-and-governance/skills/ai-capability-roadmap`

**When to use:** when an AI opportunity needs to move from an idea to an
approved, governance-compliant implementation plan in a controlled way.
